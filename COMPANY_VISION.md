# Ecodash: Maximizing Ecosystem Services of Earth

## Mission Statement
**Cultivate thriving ecosystems across the planet through computational ecology and human creativity.**

## Vision Statement
We envision landscapes where native plants support a bustling symphony of pollinators, where every garden contributes to planetary health, and where beauty emerges from ecological abundance. By uniting scientific modeling with design innovation, we transform how humanity shapes the living world.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. **Beauty Through Ecological Function**
The most profound artistry emerges when landscapes truly come alive: seasonal blooms drawing waves of pollinators, native grasses moving in wind, birds nesting in carefully chosen shrubs... This is our north star: creating beauty that arises from thriving ecological communities.

### 2. **Scientific Foundation for Living Design**
We begin with fundamental understanding—how water and nutrients move through soils, how light filters through canopies across seasons, how temperature varies through every microclimate. From these physical realities, we model plant growth, bloom timing, and wildlife interactions. This knowledge foundation enables landscapes that work with rather than against natural processes.

### 3. **Native Plants as High-Performance Solutions**
Local species offer superior wildlife value, climate adaptation, and site compatibility. They're the result of millennia of evolutionary refinement. We quantify these benefits and connect designers with the growers who preserve this genetic heritage.

### 4. **Computational Power for Ecological Complexity**
Modern computing can process the countless interactions that create thriving ecosystems. By modeling microclimates, predicting phenology, and simulating plant communities, we make nature's complexity navigable for designers.

## What We Build

### 3D Ecological Digital Twins
**Photorealistic ecophysiology simulators that show landscapes across seasons**
- Model every microclimate variation across a property
- Predict bloom sequences and pollinator visitation patterns  
- Visualize mature plant communities before installation
- Quantify ecosystem services delivered over time

### Design Intelligence Platform
**Tools that translate ecological science into actionable design**
- Plant selection optimized for site conditions and wildlife value
- Spatial arrangements that maximize beneficial interactions
- Seasonal interest planning with ecological function
- Performance metrics for every design decision

### Native Plant Network
**Connect ecological knowledge with commercial availability**
- Match designer needs with nursery inventory
- Track genetic diversity and regional ecotypes
- Enable direct grower-designer relationships
- Scale native plant adoption through market efficiency

## Pollinator Gardens: Our Proving Ground

We focus first on pollinator habitat because it represents both urgent need and achievable impact. Every property can provide food and shelter for bees, butterflies, and other essential insects. Through computational modeling of bloom phenology and pollinator preferences, we enable gardens that support abundance across seasons.

This focused start proves our approach. The same methods that optimize pollinator habitat will extend to other ecosystem services as we grow.
